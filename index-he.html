🏢 About TRUIT Instruments

TRUIT Instruments (Guangzhou) Co.,Ltd is a professional scientific instrument and laboratory equipment manufacturer integrating R&D, production, sales and service. We are dedicated to providing high-quality testing instruments for the coatings, painting, ink, and plastics industries.

The company has passed ISO 9001 Quality Management System, ISO 14001 Environmental Management System, and ISO 45001 Occupational Health and Safety Management System certifications.

We focus on technology R&D and intellectual property protection, with multiple invention and utility model patents. Our six major product series — Dispersion & Grinding, Raw Material Testing, Coating Testing, Lab Equipment, Environmental Aging, and Standard Consumables — are widely used in quality control, research institutes, and third-party testing laboratories worldwide.

Adhering to the philosophy of "Quality First, Customer Priority, Innovation Development", we continuously invest in R&D to provide excellent products and services to our clients globally.
